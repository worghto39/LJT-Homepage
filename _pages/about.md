I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Professor Junxian He, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, 2024 - Present
  - HKUST NLP Group, advised by Professor Junxian He
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 2020 - 2024
  - Graduated in June 2024
  - Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship
